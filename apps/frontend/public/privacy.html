Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

SponsCRM (“SponsCRM”, “we”, “us”) is a web application at https://www.sponscrm.tech that helps student and event teams track sponsorship outreach: the companies they contact, the people at those companies, and the progress of each conversation. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you use SponsCRM you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Your name and email address.
  • If you sign up with a password, a password hash. We never store or see your actual password.
  • If you sign in with Google, your Google account identifier, email address and name, as provided by Google Sign-In.

Organization information

  • The name of each organization (workspace) you create or join, its invite code, and your role in it (Admin, Senior or Member). One account can belong to several organizations.

Content you add to SponsCRM

  • Companies and contact details you enter or import: company names, contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, LinkedIn links, sector, status, sponsorship amounts, notes and follow-up dates.
  • Activity you log on a company (calls, emails, meetings, status changes) and the notifications we show you about assignments and milestones.
  • This content may include personal information about people who work at the companies you contact. You are responsible for having a legitimate reason to store it.

Files and sheets you import

  • When you upload an Excel or CSV file, it is read in your browser. Only the company rows you choose to import are sent to us; the file itself is not uploaded or kept.
  • When you paste a Google Sheets link, our server downloads that sheet (only if it is shared as “Anyone with the link can view”) and passes it to your browser so you can review it before importing. We do not sign in to your Google account for this, and we do not keep the sheet; rows are stored only if you choose to import them as companies.

Technical information

  • Our servers keep standard request logs (such as IP address, time, and the page or API called) to run, secure and debug the service.
  • We use a secure, HTTP-only cookie to keep you signed in, and your browser’s local storage to remember your light or dark theme. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies or trackers.

2. Google user data

SponsCRM uses Google in two ways. In each case we request only what the feature needs.

Sign in with Google

Google Sign-In gives us your basic profile: your Google account ID, email address and name. We use them only to create and recognize your SponsCRM account. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data through sign-in.

Syncing your organization’s data to a Google Sheet (Admins only)

An organization Admin can connect a Google account to export the organization’s company list to a Google Sheet. This asks for one permission: access to the files that SponsCRM itself creates in Google Drive (the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file).

  • What we do with it: when the Admin chooses “Sync”, we create one spreadsheet in the connected account and update it with the organization’s company list. With this permission SponsCRM cannot see, open or change any other file in the Google account.
  • What we store: the connection’s tokens, on our servers against that organization, so the Admin does not have to reconnect each time, and the ID of the spreadsheet we created.
  • Your control: you can remove SponsCRM’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by reconnecting with a different account.

Importing from a Google Sheets link

This does not use Google sign-in or any Google permission. It works only for sheets their owners have shared as “Anyone with the link can view”, which our server downloads the same way a browser would. Private sheets are never accessed; download them from Google Sheets and upload the file instead.

SponsCRM’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide the Sync feature above or to comply with the law, and no person at SponsCRM reads it except where you ask for support, it is needed for security or abuse investigation, or the law requires it.

3. How we use information

  • To provide SponsCRM: sign you in, show your organization’s companies and activity, assign and track work, import and export data, and send in-app notifications.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, fix problems and improve reliability.
  • To contact you about your account or this policy when needed.

4. Who we share it with

  • Members of your organization. What you add to an organization is visible to that organization’s members according to their roles. If you belong to several organizations, each organization only sees its own data.
  • Service providers that host SponsCRM for us: our website host (Vercel), our application host (Render) and our database provider (Neon). They process data only to run the service for us.
  • Google, when you sign in with Google or an Admin uses Sync, as they direct.
  • Legal reasons. If the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • We keep your information while your account exists and while it is needed to provide the service.
  • When an Admin deletes an organization, that organization’s companies, activity and notifications are deleted. When you leave an organization, your membership is removed; the companies you added stay with the organization.
  • To delete your account, or to ask us to delete personal information you believe we hold, email hussaintaherkagalwala5253@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time. Backups may keep copies for a short period before they are overwritten.

6. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, HTTP-only session cookies and role-based access limits within each organization. No online service can be perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit the information in your account, disconnect Google at any time, and ask us to correct, export or delete your information. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law; contact us and we will help.

8. Children

SponsCRM is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SponsCRM changes. We will change the “Last updated” date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app or by email.

10. Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy or your data: hussaintaherkagalwala5253@gmail.com.
